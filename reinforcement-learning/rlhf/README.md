# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

**Author:** JasonLuo  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Domain:** Reinforcement Learning

## Overview

An interactive study group presentation connecting reinforcement learning in
games to language-model alignment. It follows a response through supervised
fine-tuning (SFT), reward modeling, and PPO, with worked examples of token-level
rewards, KL regularization, and generalized advantage estimation (GAE).

The presentation also compares PPO-based RLHF, Direct Preference Optimization
(DPO), and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), and explains outcome
reward models (ORM) and process reward models (PRM).
