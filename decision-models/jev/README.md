# JEV Explained

**Author:** SteveKo837

**Date:** 2026-10-13

**Domain:** Decision Models

## Overview

A 28-slide English-language presentation, with Traditional Chinese presenter
notes, about JEV and bounded decision models. It separates JEV's public product
contract from undisclosed architecture, traces one Laya request from
serialization to option probabilities, reviews the public `typed-decisions`
fine-tuning experiment, and examines calibration, evaluation, and deployment
risk through a local convenience-store benchmark.
